SKMedKART V5.9.73 — CANONICAL PURCHASE → SALES → REMAINING STOCK

This release fixes the stock calculation at ledger level.

1. Each exact Product ID + Batch is calculated independently.
2. Purchase quantity comes from Purchase History records for that batch.
3. Sales quantity comes from non-returned bills using unique bill.id.
4. Remaining = explicit opening stock + purchase qty - sold qty + explicit stock adjustments.
5. If a batch has no Purchase History, no fake purchase is created; its existing physical stock is preserved.
6. Product stock is the sum of its batch remaining stock by Product ID. Same-name products are NOT merged.
7. Current stock is NOT reduced merely because a batch is expired. Expiry remains a separate alert.
8. Purchase History is never recovered, re-dated, filtered out, or rewritten by stock reconciliation.
9. Report Purchase Value uses the stored Purchase History directly; no recovered-record filter is applied.
10. Duplicate invoice numbers do not merge bills.
11. Service worker cache is v5.9.73 and fetches admin.js with no-store to prevent stale code.

Core rules:
1. Purchase History is the inbound ledger; reconciliation never creates/recover/re-dates purchases.
2. Bills are unique by bill.id, never by invoiceNumber.
3. Purchase-backed batch stock = explicit opening stock + purchase qty - non-returned billed qty + explicit adjustments.
4. Returned bills are not counted as sales.
5. Normal app load does not reconcile or mutate stock.
6. Reconciliation never creates synthetic batches.
7. Product stock is recalculated from actual batch stock by product ID.
8. New invoices use a free SKM-### number; existing historical invoice numbers are preserved.
9. Editing an old purchase preserves its original date unless the user changes it.
10. Blank supplier during edit does not erase an existing supplier.
11. Purchase search searches medicine, supplier, invoice, batch, manufacturer, date, category and schedule.
12. Reminder completion stores a bounded completion history.
13. Opening-stock batches are explicitly marked as MANUAL opening stock.
14. Service worker does not dynamically rewrite admin.js and clears old SKMedKART admin caches.

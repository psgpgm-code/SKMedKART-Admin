SKMedKART V5.9.70 — PURCHASE → SALES → REMAINING STOCK FINAL

ROOT METHOD
- Purchase Entry is the inbound ledger.
- Every unique bill.id is one sale transaction; invoice number is display/reference only.
- Returned bills are excluded from sales.
- Batch remaining = explicit opening stock + purchase quantity - sold quantity. Legacy stored batch.stock and legacy PURCHASE/SALE movement rows are not added.
- No purchase history is ever invented, recovered, or re-dated.

ONE-TIME LOCAL MIGRATION
- Runs automatically once in offline mode using marker skm_stock_reconciled_v14.
- Purchase-backed products are rebuilt from valid Purchase Entry rows and actual bill IDs.
- Records explicitly marked recovered=true are removed from Purchase History because they were generated by the old unsafe recovery routine.
- Products with no purchase history keep their existing product.stock as legacy opening stock; no fake purchase is created.
- Duplicate legacy batch rows are consolidated by Product ID + Batch Number for purchase-backed inventory.
- Product stock is recalculated from the resulting valid batch ledger for purchase-backed products.
- This prevents old synthetic/duplicate batches from inflating stock.

REPORT
- Purchase Value uses only valid Purchase Entry rows (recovered rows excluded), filtered by the selected purchase date range.
- Existing bill dates remain unchanged.

SKMedKART V5.9.68 — PURCHASE → SALES → REMAINING STOCK LEDGER FIX

This release fixes the root inventory calculation instead of trusting the stored batch.stock value.

CANONICAL METHOD
1. Purchase History is the inbound quantity source.
2. Every unique bill.id is counted as a separate sale transaction. Invoice number is display-only.
3. Returned bills are excluded from sales.
4. Batch remaining = Opening Stock + Purchase Qty - Sold Qty + explicit Stock Adjustment.
5. Legacy PURCHASE/SALE movement rows are NOT used for the normal stock calculation, so duplicated old movements cannot inflate stock.
6. Existing batch records are consolidated by Product ID + Batch Number. Duplicate legacy batch rows no longer increase the batch count.
7. If a historical bill has a missing batch record but a matching Purchase History row exists, the operational batch is rebuilt from that existing purchase record.
8. If a batch has no purchase history, its existing quantity is treated as legacy/unrecorded opening stock; no purchase record is invented.
9. Product stock is the sum of its calculated batch balances, by Product ID.
10. Normal app load performs a one-time V5.9.68 migration when needed, then does not continuously rewrite stock.

TRANSACTION UPDATES
- Saving a bill recalculates the ledger immediately.
- Saving/editing a purchase recalculates the ledger immediately.
- Deleting a purchase recalculates the ledger immediately.
- Returning a bill recalculates the ledger immediately.
- Existing purchase dates are preserved; new bills use today's date.
- Existing supplier is not erased by a blank edit field.
